Human Resources Team
Vicanpas is formed by a multidisciplinary team with more than 30 years of experience at your service.


Our passion is the utilization of raw materials and efficiency, achieving the conversion of rock into final product with the least impact on the environment.

For this we investigate the best and most efficient systems of production, transport and efficiency of the factories of our clients.

To accomplish this we make 110cm depth blocks which makes them ideal for transport and cutting in the new and increasingly used multi-wire machines.
This avoids the need for machinery of excessive tonnage and power, safety in road transport, avoidance of breakdowns in the trucks that transport them and optimization of time and energy by cutting the whole block in a single descent.
